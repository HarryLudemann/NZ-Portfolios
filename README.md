# NZ Portfolios 🇳🇿

A curated list of personal websites built by New Zealanders — devs, tinkerers,
and side-project people. Think portfolio sites, project blogs, and digital
gardens, not agencies or company sites.

Inspired by the itch of wanting to find more people doing this kind of thing
in NZ and not finding a good list anywhere.

## Why this exists

Personal sites like these are scattered and barely indexed. This is just an
attempt to make them a little more discoverable and maybe nudge more people
to build one.
