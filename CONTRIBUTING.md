# Contributing

Thanks for wanting to add someone (or yourself) to the list!

## Criteria

- Must be an **individual's personal site** — not a business, agency, or
  freelance-for-hire landing page.
- Must have **something built on it** — projects, tools, a blog, writing.
  A single-page CV/resume alone doesn't count.
- Should have a genuine connection to New Zealand — living here, from here,
  or work that's NZ-focused. Mention this briefly in your PR description;
  we're not going to verify it, just trusting people to be honest.

## How to add an entry

1. Fork the repo.
2. Add a row to the table in `README.md`, keeping alphabetical order by name:

| Your Name | yoursite.com | One-line description |

3. Keep the description short — under ~10 words, focus on *what's actually there*.
4. In your PR description, add a line on your NZ connection (e.g. "based in
   Hamilton" or "grew up in NZ, now overseas").
5. Open a PR.

## What gets rejected

- Agency / business / "hire me" sites with no personal projects
- Broken links or sites that are just a LinkedIn redirect
- Spam / SEO-bait additions

Low-effort or clearly promotional PRs will be closed without much explanation
— nothing personal, just keeping the list useful.
